      Questions

      Good to know

      How late can I reserve for today?

      Until 1pm, while stock lasts. Anything sold out today can be pre-ordered for tomorrow.

      What if I'm late to pick up?

      We hold reserved orders until closing. After that, unclaimed bread goes to our local food bank.

      Do you make gluten-free bread?

      Yes, a gluten-free loaf and a flourless chocolate cake every day. They're baked first, on cleaned trays, but in a kitchen that uses wheat.

      How far ahead should I order a cake?

      At least three days. For two-tier cakes or busy weekends, a week is safer. The date picker only shows dates we can do.

      Can I change or cancel a cake request?

      Yes, up to 48 hours before collection. Just reply to your confirmation email.
